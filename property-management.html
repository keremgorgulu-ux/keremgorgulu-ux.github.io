Owner FAQ

Good questions deserve clear answers.

What types of properties can you help manage?

The initial focus is residential rental homes in North Texas, especially single-family homes and similar long-term rentals. Share your property type and location in the form below so fit and service availability can be confirmed.

Can you help if my property is currently occupied?

Yes. An occupied property may require a careful transition involving the current lease, security-deposit records, resident communication, keys, maintenance history, and payment status. These details are reviewed before a transition plan is proposed.

How do you determine the recommended rent?

The rental review considers nearby comparable rentals, current competition, property condition, location, features, and timing. The goal is a market-aware recommendation—not an automated estimate detached from the neighborhood.

How are tenants screened?

Screening follows consistent written criteria and may include identity, income, credit, rental history, and other legally permissible checks. Final criteria and process details are explained before leasing begins and are applied in line with fair housing requirements.

How much does property management cost?

Pricing depends on the property, service scope, lease status, and support required. Request a rental review for a straightforward conversation about fit, expected services, and fees before making a decision.

Can you help me decide whether to rent or sell?

Yes. Because KG Realty also works with buyers and sellers, you can compare the practical considerations of holding the home as a rental versus selling it, based on your goals and current market conditions.
